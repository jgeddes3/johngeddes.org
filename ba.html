Browse Availability

When using our Browse Availability page, please keep in mind:

  • Capacity: The name of each hut is followed by that hut’s maximum capacity in parenthesis.
  • Single-Party: These huts require that you book all of the spaces, also referred to as a whole-hut booking. Your group size must be no greater than the hut’s capacity.
  • Multi-Party: These huts allow you to book by the space. If you book fewer than all spaces available, your group will likely be sharing the hut with other guests – including the communal sleeping areas.
  • Spaces Available: A number indicates that there are spaces available to reserve on the date displayed.
  • Booked or Closed: A dash “-“ indicates the hut is totally booked or closed on the date displayed.
